GameSense uses the Commons-supplied portrait/thumbnail and resizes it to fit the panel while preserving aspect ratio. It makes no other edits. Existing crops belong to the source image history. Photo metadata and licence links appear in the app's portrait credits and remain available offline. A second Hamilton cache entry maps the same photograph to the alternative Jolpica stable identifier.

This library contains six distinct photographs. Having a licensed portrait does not imply that a statistical archive for that athlete has been downloaded. Other athletes are resolved on demand only when a unique matching Wikidata identity and acceptable Commons licence can be verified. Missing or ambiguous portraits use original GameSense silhouette artwork.
